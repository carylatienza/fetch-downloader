## FAQ Page `/faq`

### Hero

**Eyebrow Label:**
> FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Headline:**
> Everything you need to know.

**Body:**
> Quick answers to common questions about Fetch.

---

### General Questions

**Q: What is Fetch?**
> Fetch is a free, web-based tool that lets you download videos and images from major social media platforms. Just paste a URL and download the media at the best available quality.

**Q: Is Fetch free?**
> Yes, completely free. No hidden costs, no premium tiers, no ads.

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**
> No. Fetch doesn't require any registration or sign-up. Just visit the site and start downloading.

**Q: Is Fetch safe to use?**
> Yes. Fetch doesn't install anything on your device, doesn't run scripts in the background, and doesn't store any personal data. What you download is what you get — just the media file.

---

### Platform Questions

**Q: Which platforms does Fetch support?**
> Fetch currently supports YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and X (formerly Twitter). We plan to add more platforms in the future.

**Q: Can I download Instagram stories?**
> Yes, as long as the story is from a public account.

**Q: Can I download YouTube Shorts?**
> Yes, YouTube Shorts are fully supported.

**Q: Can I download private or restricted content?**
> No. Fetch can only access content that is publicly available. If a video or image is behind a private account or restricted settings, we can't extract it.

**Q: What about playlists or multiple videos?**
> Currently, Fetch supports one URL at a time. Playlist and batch download support are planned for a future update.

---

### Technical Questions

**Q: What quality will I get?**
> Fetch always downloads the highest quality available. For YouTube, this can be up to 4K (2160p). Quality depends on what the original uploader posted.

**Q: What file format are downloads?**
> Videos download as MP4 files. Images download as JPG or PNG, depending on the original format.

**Q: Is there a file size limit?**
> Downloads are capped at 2GB per file. Most social media videos are well under this limit.

**Q: Why did my download fail?**
> Downloads can fail if the content has been removed, made private, or if the platform has changed something temporarily. Try again in a few minutes. If the problem persists, the content may no longer be available.

**Q: Can I download on mobile?**
> Fetch works in mobile browsers, but the download experience depends on your device and browser. Desktop browsers provide the most reliable experience.

---

### Legal Questions

**Q: Is it legal to download videos from social media?**
> Downloading publicly available content for personal use is generally permitted in most jurisdictions. However, redistributing, re-uploading, or using downloaded content commercially without the creator's permission may violate copyright laws. Always respect content creators' rights.

**Q: Does Fetch store or host downloaded content?**
> No. Fetch doesn't store any media on its servers. Content is extracted and streamed directly to your browser. Nothing is saved on our end after the download completes.

**Q: What about DMCA requests?**
> Fetch respects intellectual property rights. If you are a content creator and believe your work is being misused through our service, please contact us and we will address the concern promptly.

> Fetch is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

> **The short version:** We collect almost nothing. We don't require accounts, we don't track you, and we don't store your downloads.

**2. Information We Collect**

> **Information We Don't Collect:**
> - We do not collect personal information (name, email, phone number).
> - We do not require user accounts or registration.
> - We do not store downloaded media on our servers.
> - We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising.
>
> **Information That May Be Collected Automatically:**
> - Standard server logs (IP address, timestamp, requested URL) — these are maintained by our hosting provider for security and operational purposes.
> - Request metadata necessary to process your download (the URL you submit and the media metadata extracted from it). This data is processed in memory and discarded after your download completes.

**3. How We Use Information**

> Server logs are used solely for:
> - Maintaining the security and integrity of the Service.
> - Diagnosing technical issues.
> - Enforcing rate limits to prevent abuse.
>
> We do not sell, share, or use log data for marketing or analytics purposes.

**4. Data Retention**

> - **Download data:** Processed in memory only. No media content or download metadata is stored after the request completes.
> - **Server logs:** Retained by our hosting provider (Render.com) according to their standard retention policies.

> Fetch is hosted on Render.com. When you use Fetch, your requests pass through Render's infrastructure. Render's privacy policy applies to their handling of server logs and infrastructure data.
